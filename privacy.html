Audrey Radar Sync

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 20, 2026

Audrey Radar Sync is a personal-use application for synchronizing research reports and related files between the application owner's computer and Google Drive.

Information accessed

With the owner's Google OAuth authorization, the application accesses Google Drive files within the permissions granted by the owner.

How information is used

Google Drive access is used to upload, retrieve, and manage research-related files for the owner's personal use. The application is not a public service.

Sharing

The application does not sell Google user data or intentionally share it with advertisers or unrelated third parties. Files remain subject to any sharing settings the owner applies in Google Drive.

Storage and security

Research files are stored on the owner's computer and in the owner's Google Drive account. OAuth credentials are stored in the local rclone configuration and should be protected from unauthorized access.

Retention and deletion

The owner controls file retention and can delete synchronized files in Google Drive or locally. The owner may revoke OAuth access in Google Account security settings.

Changes and contact

This policy may be updated if the application's behavior changes. For inquiries, use the support email displayed on the application's Google OAuth consent screen.
